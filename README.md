### How do I choose between document automation and basic e-signature tools?

Basic e-signature tools work if you're only sending pre-written contracts for signature, but document automation platforms save significant time when you're creating proposals, quotes, or contracts from scratch repeatedly. If you're copying data from your CRM into documents manually or customizing pricing more than a few times weekly, automation pays for itself quickly through time savings.

### Can these platforms integrate with my existing CRM?

Most major document platforms integrate with Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and other popular CRMs, syncing contact data automatically and updating deal stages when contracts are signed. Check the specific integration marketplace for your CRM before committing to ensure the connection supports your required workflows.

### What's the typical ROI timeline for document automation?

Most businesses see payback within 2-4 months through reduced document creation time, faster signature turnaround, and improved close rates. Teams report saving hours daily that previously went to copying data, chasing signatures, and fixing errors in manually created documents.
